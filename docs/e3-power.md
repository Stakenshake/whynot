# E3 — Statistical power

**Result: both main claims hold at n=20 across three model families, with
p < 0.0001. The effect is strong but, for one decision, not total.**

This run also produces the most useful refinement in the whole project: what
matters is not the age of a decision, it is whether its stated **reason** looks
like a condition that could have changed.

---

## Design

Two experiments, three families, n=20 per cell.

| | E3a | E3b |
|---|---|---|
| Task | Add session storage to a Node/Express SaaS | Choose a stack for a new reporting service |
| Decision | "sessions not in Redis" — 1 month old, concrete failure | "do not use TypeScript" — 7 years old, reason is team skill |
| Control | no record | no record |
| Treatment | the record, injected | the record, injected |

Models:

| Label | Family | Route |
|---|---|---|
| `gpt-6.1-sol` | OpenAI | codex |
| `ds-v4.1-flash` | DeepSeek | workbuddy |
| `haiku-5.5` | Anthropic | opencode-go |

Context was generated by the real `decisions_check` tool. **A preflight asserts
that the returned text contains the decision's title** — a check added after the
first E3 run silently injected "nothing found" because the query used 存储 while
the decision said 存.

Judged by Kimi K3, with DeepSeek Flash as fallback. **240 responses, 0 errors.**

---

## E3a — recording a rejection stops re-proposal

| Model | Control | Treatment | Risk difference | Fisher p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPT-6.1-Sol | 20/20 | **0/20** | −100 pts | <0.0001 |
| DeepSeek V4.1 Flash | 20/20 | **0/20** | −100 pts | <0.0001 |
| Claude Haiku 5.5 | 12/20 | **0/20** | −60 pts | <0.0001 |
| **Pooled** | **52/60** | **0/60** | **−87 pts** | **<0.0001** |

Pre-registered rule: pooled p < 0.01 and |risk difference| ≥ 0.40.

**MAIN CLAIM STANDS.** Treatment is 0 out of 60 — the decision was followed
every single time.

Note the baseline: Haiku only recommended Redis 12/20 times without a record.
The models do not start from the same place, which is why pooled and per-model
numbers are both reported.

## E3b — a stale decision is followed, but not always

| Model | Control | Treatment | Risk difference | Fisher p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPT-6.1-Sol | 20/20 | **0/20** | −100 pts | <0.0001 |
| DeepSeek V4.1 Flash | 20/20 | **6/20** | −70 pts | <0.0001 |
| Claude Haiku 5.5 | 20/20 | **7/20** | −65 pts | <0.0001 |
| **Pooled** | **60/60** | **13/60** | **−78 pts** | **<0.0001** |

**MAIN CLAIM STANDS**, but 13 of 60 responses still recommended TypeScript.

That is materially different from the "6 of 6 blindly obeyed" phrasing in the
first write-up. The honest statement is:

> A stale decision shifts the answer by roughly 78 percentage points, and
> GPT obeys it completely. One in five responses resists it anyway.

---

## Why the resistance happens — and what it corrects

The resistant responses do not ignore the decision. They **attack its premise**:

> "它的成立前提是『团队没人会写 TS、编译流程要额外配置』，而这个前提现在大概率已不成立——2019 到现在的 Node 生态里，TS 已是默认选项"
>
> "它的拒绝理由是当时的人员和交付约束，**不是技术判断**"
>
> "决策给出的前提条件（团队不会 TS）在过去 7 年里很可能已经不成立了"

### This explains a discrepancy with E4

E4 used the same title with a different reason:

> "企业报表服务的字段和接口变更频繁，但引入 TypeScript 需要额外的编译与构建配置；当时判断这会让交付变慢。"

That reason reads as a **property of the project**. The E3b reason reads as a
**snapshot of a team**. Same title, same age, different resistance:

| Reason phrasing | Reads as | Resistance |
|---|---|---|
| "the team did not know TS" | changeable condition | **13/60 (22%)** |
| "this project's fields change constantly" | project property | **0/60 in E4's comparable cells** |

### Corrected model

The earlier conclusion — "obedience is age-independent" — is directionally
right but incomplete. The fuller model:

1. **Age is not used.** A 30-day note and a 7-year note have identical authority
   (E4).
2. **The stated reason is used.** If the reason is recognisably about a
   changeable condition, some models will challenge it.
3. **The framing of the reason therefore matters more than the date.**

This has a direct product implication:

> Writing "团队没人会 TS" is *safer* than writing "这个项目的字段变更频繁",
> because the first invites the model to re-check it and the second does not.

That is the opposite of the usual advice to write durable, timeless rationales.
For this tool, a reason that names a **condition** is more useful than one that
states a **property**.

---

## What this changes in the public claim

The README and launch material should not say "6 of 6 blindly obey". The
defensible sentence is:

> Recording a decision changes what the agent proposes, by roughly 78–87
> percentage points. A stale decision still shifts answers by about 78 points,
> and one model obeyed it completely. Roughly one in five responses resists —
> and when they resist, they do it by attacking the stated reason, which only
> works if the reason names a condition rather than a property.

---

## Caveats

- Three families, but DeepSeek is Flash (the smaller variant). A larger DeepSeek
  model may resist differently.
- Single-turn, synthetic task, no code context. E1 addresses the code-context
  confound.
- Pooling across models hides heterogeneity. The per-model rows are the
  primary data; the pooled row is a summary.
- The E3a and E3b decisions differ in more than age (reason phrasing, domain).
  Do not read the two experiments as a controlled comparison of age — that is
  what E4 was for.
